# prdctv privacy policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

prdctv does not collect, sell or share any personal data.

## What the app stores

Your tasks, tags, rewards and settings are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, prdctv also keeps them in your private iCloud database so they sync between your devices. That database belongs to your iCloud account. The developer can't see or access it.

## What the app doesn't do

prdctv has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. It doesn't use third party services that collect data, and it doesn't contact any server other than Apple's iCloud.

## Photos

The whiteboard import reads a photo you choose to find task titles. The text is read on your device, and the photo isn't saved or uploaded.

## Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove its data from a device. To remove synced data from iCloud, open Settings, then your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage, and delete the prdctv data.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the support link on the App Store page.
