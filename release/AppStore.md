## Description (4000)

prdctv replaces the need to do and want to do lists on your whiteboard with one board.

Put tasks in Up Next, move them to In Progress, and finish them in Done. Need tasks earn points, and every few points earns a Want or Fun reward. Want and Fun tasks stay locked until you spend a reward on them.

Board
• Up Next, In Progress and Done columns, with drag and drop on Mac
• Group each column by category or by tag
• Points on a 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 scale, or turn points off and count tasks
• Due dates, notes and a month calendar you can drag tasks across
• History of past weeks and stats with streaks

Tags
• Tag tasks by class, project or topic, such as Chemistry
• Each tag keeps one color, which you can change
• Show one tag at a time, or group every column by tag

Rewards
• Choose how many need points earn a Want or Fun reward
• Turn rewards off if you only want the board

Widgets
• See your board and reward meter on the desktop or Home Screen
• Check off tasks from the widget
• List tasks under their tags, with untagged tasks at the end

Quick add
• Type a task in one line with points, tags and a due date, such as "read chapter 4 3 #history !fri"
• A global shortcut on Mac opens quick add from any app
• Import a photo of your whiteboard and turn each line into a task

Make it yours
• Pick a color and icon for Need, Want and Fun, and colors for each column
• Use light mode, dark mode or your system setting

Your tasks sync between your Mac, iPhone and iPad through your own iCloud account. prdctv has no accounts, ads or tracking.

## Notes for App Review

No sign in is needed, and the app starts empty. Add a few Need tasks and move them to Done, and a Want reward appears after 4 points. Want and Fun tasks are locked until a reward is spent, which is intended. iCloud sync uses the reviewer's own iCloud account and works without setup. On Mac, the app adds a menu bar icon, and Option Space opens quick add from any app. The shortcut can be changed in Settings. Rewards and points can each be turned off in Settings, and widget layout options are in Settings under Widget.
