prdctv support

prdctv is a task board for Mac, iPhone and iPad. Finish Need tasks to earn Want and Fun rewards.

Questions, bugs or ideas

support@example.com

If something went wrong, include your device, its system version and what you were doing.

My tasks don't appear on my other devices

prdctv syncs through your iCloud account. Make sure each device is signed in to the same iCloud account and that prdctv is allowed to use iCloud. New changes can take a minute to appear.

How do I change what the widget shows?

Open prdctv, go to Settings and find the Widget section. List by tag, Show miscellaneous and the reward meter on the large widget are there. To add the widget, use the widget gallery on your Mac desktop or Home Screen.

Can I use prdctv without rewards or points?

Yes. Turn off Want and Fun rewards or Points in Settings. Your tasks and history are kept and come back if you turn them on again.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to remove its data from a device. To remove the copy synced to iCloud, open Settings on iPhone or System Settings on Mac, choose your name, then iCloud, and delete the prdctv data from your iCloud storage.
